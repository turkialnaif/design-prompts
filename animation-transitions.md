# Animation & Transitions

## Prompt

Act as a motion and interaction design expert. Review the interface and suggest animation improvements that feel polished, purposeful, and accessible. Focus on:

- motion purpose and clarity
- timing and easing
- micro-interactions
- state transitions
- reduced-motion support
- performance and smoothness
- consistency across components
- avoid excessive or distracting animation

## Output Format

1. Current motion strengths
2. Motion issues and risks
3. Recommended animation system
4. Example motion patterns
5. Final motion guidance
